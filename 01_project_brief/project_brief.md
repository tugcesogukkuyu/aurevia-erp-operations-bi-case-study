# Aurevia ERP Operations & BI Dashboard Case Study

## Project Brief

### 1. Project Background

Aurevia Spa & Wellness was initially designed as a digital wellness brand with a service-oriented website, appointment booking flow, admin panel, automation support, and AI-assisted competitor analysis.

As a second phase, the business scenario has been expanded into a B2B operational model under the name **Aurevia Professional Supply**.

In this extended business model, Aurevia supplies professional spa and wellness products to hotels, spa centers, beauty salons, clinics, and premium wellness businesses. This creates a need for structured ERP-based management of customers, suppliers, products, purchasing, inventory, sales orders, invoicing, payments, and operational reporting.

This case study focuses on modeling that operational structure through an ERP portal, SQL Server database design, Power BI reporting, UAT documentation, and user request analysis.

---

### 2. Business Scenario

Aurevia Professional Supply operates as a B2B supplier for the wellness and spa industry.

The company sells:

* Professional massage oils
* Aromatherapy products
* Hammam and ritual kits
* Spa textile products
* Skincare and facial care products
* Disposable spa consumables
* Premium wellness packages
* Staff training and consulting services

The company works with multiple customer types, suppliers, product categories, warehouses, payment terms, and sales channels.

As the business grows, manual tracking through spreadsheets becomes insufficient. The company needs a centralized ERP structure to manage operational processes and transform ERP-generated data into business intelligence reports.

---

### 3. Project Objective

The main objective of this project is to demonstrate how ERP-based operational processes can be designed, executed, analyzed, tested, and reported in a business context.

The project covers the following areas:

* ERP portal usage for operational transactions
* Customer, supplier, product, and warehouse master data setup
* Sales, purchasing, inventory, invoicing, and payment workflows
* SQL Server-based relational data modeling
* Synthetic ERP transaction dataset creation
* Power BI dashboard development
* UAT and Go-Live readiness documentation
* User request and issue tracking
* Business KPI analysis for management reporting

The goal is not only to create a dashboard, but also to show how business processes generate operational data and how this data can be transformed into actionable management insights.

---

### 4. ERP Scope

The ERP process scope includes:

* Customer management
* Supplier management
* Product and service management
* Purchase order management
* Stock receipt and inventory movement tracking
* Sales order management
* Delivery and stock-out process
* Invoice creation
* Payment and collection tracking
* Operational reporting
* User request and issue analysis
* UAT and Go-Live preparation

---

### 5. Tools & Technologies

The project will use the following tools:

* Odoo ERP for ERP portal operations
* SQL Server for relational database modeling
* Power BI for dashboard and reporting
* Excel / CSV for synthetic data preparation
* Draw.io / Canva / Figma for process flow diagrams
* GitHub for documentation and portfolio presentation

---

### 6. Targeted Business Value

This case study is designed to demonstrate the ability to:

* Understand ERP workflows from an operational perspective
* Use ERP modules for real business processes
* Translate business processes into structured data models
* Analyze user needs and operational issues
* Prepare UAT test scenarios and Go-Live support documentation
* Build Power BI dashboards based on ERP-related data
* Communicate technical and business outputs clearly to decision makers

---

### 7. Portfolio Positioning

This project is positioned as a professional ERP operations and business intelligence case study.

It connects digital product development, business analysis, ERP process understanding, data modeling, operational support, and reporting skills in a single end-to-end business scenario.
