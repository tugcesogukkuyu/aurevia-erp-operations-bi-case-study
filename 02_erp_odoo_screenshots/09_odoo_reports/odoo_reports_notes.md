# Odoo ERP Reporting Notes

This step documents the ERP reporting outputs captured in Odoo for Aurevia Professional Supply.

## 1. Reporting Purpose

The purpose of this step is to demonstrate that ERP transactions created in Odoo can generate operational reporting outputs.

The completed ERP workflow includes:

* Purchase order creation
* Stock receipt
* Sales order creation
* Customer delivery / stock-out
* Customer invoice creation
* Payment registration

These transactions create operational data that can be reviewed through Odoo reporting screens and later modeled in SQL Server for Power BI reporting.

---

## 2. Sales Reporting

A sales reporting screen was captured from Odoo to demonstrate sales analysis based on the completed customer sales order.

The sales report reflects the Order-to-Cash transaction created for Lunara Hotel & Spa.

### Reporting relevance

The sales report supports:

* Customer-based sales analysis
* Product-based sales analysis
* Revenue tracking
* Sales order performance review
* ERP-to-BI reporting preparation

---

## 3. Inventory Reporting

An inventory reporting screen was captured from Odoo to demonstrate stock visibility after purchase and delivery operations.

The inventory flow includes:

* 500 units purchased from Botanica Oils Ltd.
* 500 units received into stock
* 120 units delivered to Lunara Hotel & Spa
* Remaining stock available for future sales operations

### Reporting relevance

The inventory report supports:

* Stock-on-hand analysis
* Inventory movement tracking
* Purchase-to-stock validation
* Stock-out analysis
* Reorder risk monitoring
* Product availability reporting

---

## 4. ERP-to-BI Relevance

Odoo reporting screens are used as operational proof of ERP portal usage.

The next phase of the project will extend this ERP logic into a larger synthetic SQL Server dataset for professional-scale Power BI dashboard development.

This supports the transition from operational ERP usage to business intelligence reporting.
