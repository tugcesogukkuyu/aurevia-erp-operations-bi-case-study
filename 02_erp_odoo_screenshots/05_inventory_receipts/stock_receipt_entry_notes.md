# Stock Receipt Entry Notes

This step documents the inventory receipt process completed in the Odoo ERP portal for Aurevia Professional Supply.

## Stock Receipt Scenario

After confirming the purchase order from Botanica Oils Ltd., Aurevia received the purchased products into inventory.

The receipt operation was completed to demonstrate how a confirmed purchase order increases available stock in the ERP system.

## Stock Receipt Details

| Field                  | Value                               |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Related Purchase Order | P00001                              |
| Supplier               | Botanica Oils Ltd.                  |
| Product                | Aurevia Lavender Massage Oil 500 ml |
| Internal Reference     | PRD-001                             |
| Received Quantity      | 500                                 |
| Receipt Type           | Vendor Receipt / Incoming Stock     |
| Inventory Impact       | Stock quantity increased            |

## ERP Purpose

This stock receipt demonstrates the following ERP capabilities:

* Receiving products from a supplier
* Linking stock receipt to a purchase order
* Increasing available inventory
* Preparing stock for future sales orders
* Supporting inventory movement tracking
* Creating operational data for stock reporting

## Business Relevance

This step completes the purchasing side of the Procure-to-Stock process.

The received inventory will be used in the next workflow stage: customer sales order creation, delivery, stock-out, invoicing and payment tracking.
