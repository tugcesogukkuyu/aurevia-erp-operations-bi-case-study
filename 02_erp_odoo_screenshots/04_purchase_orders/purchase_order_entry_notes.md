# Purchase Order Entry Notes

This step documents the first purchase order created in the Odoo ERP portal for Aurevia Professional Supply.

## Purchase Order Scenario

Aurevia Professional Supply needs to replenish inventory for one of its core products: Aurevia Lavender Massage Oil 500 ml.

A purchase order was created for the supplier Botanica Oils Ltd. to demonstrate supplier-based purchasing and stock replenishment workflow.

## Purchase Order Details

| Field                         | Value                               |
| ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Supplier                      | Botanica Oils Ltd.                  |
| Product                       | Aurevia Lavender Massage Oil 500 ml |
| Internal Reference            | PRD-001                             |
| Quantity                      | 500                                 |
| Unit Cost                     | 180                                 |
| Purchase Amount Excluding Tax | 90,000                              |
| Purchase Order Status         | Confirmed Purchase Order            |
| Receipt Status                | Not Received before stock receipt   |

## ERP Purpose

This purchase order demonstrates the following ERP capabilities:

* Supplier selection
* Purchase order creation
* Product quantity and unit cost entry
* Purchase order confirmation
* Link between purchase order and inventory receipt
* Preparation for supplier-based purchasing analysis

## Business Relevance

This step represents the Procure-to-Stock process.

The purchase order creates the operational basis for increasing inventory after supplier delivery. Once received, the stock quantity becomes available for future sales orders, delivery operations, invoicing and inventory reporting.
