# Sales Order Entry Notes

This step documents the first customer sales order created in the Odoo ERP portal for Aurevia Professional Supply.

## Sales Order Scenario

Aurevia Professional Supply created a B2B sales order for Lunara Hotel & Spa after receiving inventory from the supplier Botanica Oils Ltd.

This sales order demonstrates the Order-to-Cash workflow, starting from customer order creation and continuing through delivery, invoicing and payment collection.

## Sales Order Details

| Field                      | Value                               |
| -------------------------- | ----------------------------------- |
| Customer                   | Lunara Hotel & Spa                  |
| Product                    | Aurevia Lavender Massage Oil 500 ml |
| Internal Reference         | PRD-001                             |
| Quantity Ordered           | 120                                 |
| Unit Sales Price           | 320                                 |
| Sales Amount Excluding Tax | 38,400                              |
| Customer Segment           | Hotel & Resort                      |
| Sales Channel              | B2B Direct                          |
| Payment Term               | Net 30                              |

## ERP Purpose

This sales order demonstrates the following ERP capabilities:

* Customer selection
* Product selection
* Sales order creation
* Quantity and sales price entry
* Stock availability usage after purchase receipt
* Link between sales order, delivery, invoice and payment
* Preparation for customer, product and segment-based reporting

## Business Relevance

This step represents the beginning of the Order-to-Cash process.

The confirmed sales order creates the operational basis for delivery, stock-out, customer invoicing, payment tracking and revenue reporting.
