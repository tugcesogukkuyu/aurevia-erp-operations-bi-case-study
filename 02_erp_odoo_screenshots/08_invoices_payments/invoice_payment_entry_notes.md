# Invoice and Payment Entry Notes

This step documents the invoice creation and payment registration process completed in the Odoo ERP portal for Aurevia Professional Supply.

## Invoice Scenario

After the sales order and delivery operation were completed for Lunara Hotel & Spa, Aurevia generated a customer invoice from the sales order.

The invoice was then confirmed and payment was registered to demonstrate the financial completion of the Order-to-Cash workflow.

| Customer                     | Lunara Hotel & Spa                  |
| Product                      | Aurevia Lavender Massage Oil 500 ml |

| Unit Sales Price             | 320                                 |
| Invoice Amount Excluding Tax | 38,400                              |
| Invoice Type                 | Customer Invoice                    |

## Payment Details

| Field             | Value                   |
| ----------------- | ----------------------- |
| Payment Status    | Paid                    |

| Collection Impact | Invoice fully collected |

## ERP Purpose

This invoice and payment process demonstrates the following ERP capabilities:

* Creating customer invoice from sales order
* Posting / confirming invoice
* Registering customer payment
* Tracking paid invoice status
* Connecting sales, delivery, invoicing and payment data
* Creating financial data for revenue and collection reporting

## Business Relevance

This step completes the financial side of the Order-to-Cash process.

The completed payment record supports revenue tracking, collection rate analysis, overdue invoice monitoring and customer-based receivables reporting.
