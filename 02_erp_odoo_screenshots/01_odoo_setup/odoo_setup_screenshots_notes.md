# Odoo Setup Screenshots Notes

This folder documents the initial Odoo ERP environment setup for the Aurevia ERP Operations & BI Dashboard Case Study.

## Purpose

The purpose of these screenshots is to show that the ERP operational layer was created in Odoo for the company scenario named **Aurevia Professional Supply**.

## Captured Setup Evidence

The screenshots in this folder demonstrate:

- Odoo ERP portal access
- Aurevia Professional Supply company environment
- Installed or available ERP applications
- ERP modules used in the case study, including Sales, Purchase, Inventory, Invoicing and Reporting

## ERP Relevance

This setup is the starting point of the hands-on ERP workflow.

The following ERP operations were later performed in this environment:

- Customer and supplier master data creation
- Product and service setup
- Purchase order creation
- Stock receipt
- Sales order creation
- Delivery and stock-out
- Invoice creation
- Payment registration
- ERP reporting screen capture
