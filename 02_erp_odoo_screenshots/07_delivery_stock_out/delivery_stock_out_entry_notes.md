# Delivery and Stock-Out Entry Notes

This step documents the delivery and stock-out process completed in the Odoo ERP portal for Aurevia Professional Supply.

## Delivery Scenario

After confirming the sales order for Lunara Hotel & Spa, Aurevia processed the delivery operation for the ordered products.

The delivery operation reduced inventory quantity and completed the physical product movement from warehouse stock to the customer.

| Field                 | Value                               |

| Customer              | Lunara Hotel & Spa                  |

| Delivery Type         | Customer Delivery / Stock-Out       |

## ERP Purpose

This delivery operation demonstrates the following ERP capabilities:

* Linking customer delivery to a sales order
* Processing stock-out from available inventory
* Reducing product stock quantity after delivery
* Completing the operational fulfillment stage
* Creating inventory movement data for stock reporting

## Business Relevance

This step completes the logistics side of the Order-to-Cash process.

The delivery record supports inventory tracking, stock movement analysis, customer fulfillment reporting and future sales availability checks.
