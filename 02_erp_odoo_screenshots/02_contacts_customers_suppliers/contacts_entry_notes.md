# Contacts, Customers and Suppliers Entry Notes

This step documents the customer and supplier master data created in the Odoo ERP portal for Aurevia Professional Supply.

## 1. Purpose

The purpose of this step is to create the foundational contact master data required for ERP operations.

In Odoo, both customers and suppliers are managed as company/contact records. For this case study, customer and supplier records were separated using structured tags such as `CUSTOMER` and `SUPPLIER`.

These records will be used in sales, purchasing, invoicing, payment tracking, supplier analysis, customer segmentation and operational reporting workflows.

---

## 2. Customers Created

10 representative B2B customer records were created in Odoo.

The customer dataset includes:

* Hotels and resorts
* Spa centers
* Beauty salons
* Clinics
* Distributor / reseller account

Each customer record includes:

* Company name
* Address
* District and city
* Country
* Email
* Phone number
* Website
* Customer tags
* Customer segment
* Sales channel
* Payment term
* ERP usage notes

## Customer Tags Used

Customer records were tagged with:

* CUSTOMER
* Segment tag such as HOTEL, SPA, BEAUTY, CLINIC or DISTRIBUTOR
* Payment term tag such as Net 15, Net 30, Net 45 or Net 60
* Sales channel tag such as B2B Direct, Website Lead, Sales Representative or Distributor

---

## 3. Suppliers Created

6 supplier records were created in Odoo.

The supplier dataset includes vendors for:

* Massage oils
* Aromatherapy products
* Spa textile products
* Skincare products
* Hammam products
* Consumables and packaging

Each supplier record includes:

* Company name
* Address
* District and city
* Country
* Email
* Phone number
* Website
* Supplier tags
* Supplier category
* Payment term
* ERP usage notes

## Supplier Tags Used

Supplier records were tagged with:

* SUPPLIER
* Supplier category such as Massage Oils, Aromatherapy, Spa Textile, Skincare, Hammam Products or Consumables
* Payment term tag such as Net 30 or Net 45

---

## 4. ERP Purpose

The customer and supplier master data will be used in the following ERP workflow steps:

* Purchase order creation
* Stock receipt
* Sales order creation
* Delivery and stock-out
* Invoice generation
* Payment tracking
* Customer-based reporting
* Supplier-based purchasing analysis
* Segment and channel-based sales analysis
